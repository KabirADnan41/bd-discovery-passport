Privacy notice

Last updated 28 September 2026. গোপনীয়তা বিজ্ঞপ্তি

Bangladesh Discovery Passport is a free, non-commercial atlas game. It collects as little as possible: no names, no email addresses, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

If you play as a guest

Nothing about you is sent to our server. Your stamps and your light or dark preference are kept only in your own browser (see “Cookies and browser storage” below). Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you create an account

We store only what the account needs to work:

WhatWhyHow long
Your username (which you choose; please do not use your real name). It is never shown to anyone else. To sign you in Until you delete your account
A password check value. Your password is scrambled in your browser before it is sent, and we store only a further keyed scramble of that result, never the password itself To confirm it is you when you sign in Until you delete your account
The list of districts you have discovered, and when your account was created To show your passport on any device Until you delete your account or reset your passport
Session records (a scrambled copy of your sign-in token and its expiry) To keep you signed in 30 days, or until you sign out
Counters of recent wrong-password attempts and sign-ups, keyed by scrambled forms of your username and network address (neither is stored in readable form) To stop password guessing and automated sign-ups Removed automatically, normally within a day; removed at once when you delete your account

We rely on providing the service you asked for (your account) and on keeping it secure. We never sell or share your data, and we do not use it for anything else.

Hosting

The site and its database are hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical request data such as your IP address in order to deliver and protect the site. That processing is covered by Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Cloudflare’s network is global, so data may be handled outside your country. The database keeps automatic backups for up to 7 days, so deleted data can remain in those backups for up to 7 days before it is gone for good.

Cookies and browser storage

NameTypePurpose
__Host-bdp_session Cookie, only after you sign in; expires after 30 days Keeps you signed in. It cannot be read by scripts and is only sent to this site.
bd-discovery-passport:v1 Browser storage (guests only) Remembers a guest’s stamps on this device.
bd-discovery-passport:theme Browser storage Remembers light or dark mode if you choose one.

These are all strictly necessary for features you use, so there is no cookie banner. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

Your choices and rights

  • See your data: your username and stamps are shown in the app.
  • Change it: “Reset passport” in the menu clears your stamps.
  • Delete it: “Delete account” in the menu removes your account, stamps and sessions immediately (and from backups within 7 days).
  • Sign out at any time to end the session on that device.

Children

Anyone can explore the atlas as a guest without an account. If you are under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with a strict content security policy, passwords never leave your browser in readable form, sign-in cookies are protected from scripts and other sites, and repeated sign-in attempts are slowed down. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.
